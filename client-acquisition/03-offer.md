# 03 — The Offer

## The core promise

> **We turn fragmented eCommerce marketing into one revenue system —
> so growth stops depending on how much you spend, and starts depending on how well the
> system converts what you already have.**

Neon Mountain does not sell ads, sites, emails or content. It sells **profitable growth from
the traffic, customers and brand assets a business already has**, with execution attached.

---

## 1. Offer architecture

Three steps. Each one is a complete product that stands on its own, and each one makes the
next an obvious decision.

```
   STEP 1              STEP 2                     STEP 3
   The Growth      →   The 90-Day Sprint      →   Growth Partner
   Teardown            (rebuild the system)       (run the system)
   $2,500              $15,000                    $6,000–10,000/mo
   1 week              90 days                    Rolling, 6-mo initial
   Paid diagnostic     Fixes the top 3 leaks      Compounds the gains
   100% credited →     Credited toward retainer
```

> Prices below are the recommended structure for the $8k–20k project / $5–10k month band you
> selected. Sanity-check every number against your own delivery costs and target margin before
> publishing them.

## 2. Step 1 — The Growth Teardown ($2,500, one week)

**A paid diagnostic that finds where the money is leaking.** This is the primary conversion
event of the entire acquisition system: it converts a stranger into a paying client at a price
almost any $2M+ brand can approve without a board meeting.

**What they get:**
1. **Full-funnel audit** — Shopify analytics, Meta/Google ad accounts, Klaviyo, GA4.
2. **The Leak Report** — every identified gap across acquisition, conversion, retention and AOV,
   each one sized by estimated revenue impact **using their own data**, and ranked.
3. **The Growth Model** — a simple spreadsheet showing what their revenue and contribution
   margin look like if the top three leaks are fixed. Built on their numbers, with assumptions
   stated on the face of it.
4. **A 90-day priority roadmap** — sequenced, with owners and effort estimates.
5. **A 60-minute findings session**, recorded, with the founder and their team.

**Why it works as an entry offer**
- Removes the "is this agency any good" risk for $2,500 instead of $60,000.
- The audit *is* the sales call. You are never pitching — you are presenting findings.
- Paid diagnostics attract buyers and repel tyre-kickers. A free audit attracts the opposite.
- If they take the roadmap and implement it themselves, you keep the fee and the goodwill,
  and they come back in six months. That is an acceptable outcome, and you should say so.

**Terms:** fee credited in full against a Sprint or retainer started within 30 days.

## 3. Step 2 — The 90-Day Growth Sprint ($15,000)

**Rebuild the highest-impact parts of the customer journey.** Priced within your $8k–20k band;
scope up or down against the roadmap.

**Structure**
- **Days 1–14 — Foundation.** Tracking and attribution fixed so every later number is trustworthy.
  Single-source reporting dashboard built. Baseline metrics locked and signed off.
- **Days 15–45 — Fix the top two leaks.** Chosen from the Teardown, typically two of:
  creative/offer strategy, PDP and checkout conversion, email flow rebuild, AOV architecture.
- **Days 46–75 — Fix leak three, and build the testing cadence** that keeps the system improving
  after we leave.
- **Days 76–90 — Compound and hand over.** Results readout against baseline, documented SOPs,
  and either a scale plan or a clean exit.

**Always included**
- Weekly 30-minute working call with the founder (not a status update — a decision meeting).
- One reporting source of truth, tied to blended MER and contribution margin.
- Everything documented and owned by the client. No hostage-taking of accounts, assets or data.

**Commitment we make:** baseline metrics agreed in writing in week two, and reported against
honestly at day 90 whether they moved or not.

## 5. Why this is hard to say no to

Nine mechanisms, in the order they matter:

1. **Diagnosis before prescription.** You never quote before you know. That alone separates you
   from every agency that pitches a retainer on call one.
2. **Low-risk first purchase.** $2,500 to find out, credited if they proceed. The decision is
   small; the information is large.
3. **One accountable owner.** The Blame Loop ends. One team, one number, one throat to choke.
4. **A number they actually care about.** Contribution margin and blended MER, not platform ROAS.
5. **Their data, not our anecdotes.** Every projection is built on their accounts, with
   assumptions visible.
6. **They keep everything.** Accounts, assets, dashboards, SOPs stay in their name. No lock-in
   by hostage.
7. **Sequenced, not simultaneous.** Three leaks fixed properly beats twelve started badly.
8. **An honest exit.** Rolling after month six, 30 days' notice. The retainer survives on results.
9. **We say no.** Published disqualifiers. Agencies that take everyone are optimising for their
   own revenue, and founders know it.

## 6. Risk reversal — pick one, and only one you can honour

Do not promise a revenue outcome you cannot control. These are ordered from safest to boldest.

- **A. The Teardown guarantee (recommended).** "If the Teardown doesn't identify at least three
  specific, quantified opportunities in your business, you don't pay." Almost zero risk to you —
  a real audit always finds three — and it removes the entry barrier entirely.
- **B. The Sprint work guarantee.** "Every deliverable in the Sprint scope ships in 90 days, or
  we work free until it does." Guarantees your effort, which you control. Not their outcome.
- **C. The 30-day exit.** "If after 30 days of the Sprint you don't believe in the plan, walk
  away and pay only for work delivered."
- **D. Performance-linked pricing (advanced, use sparingly).** Base retainer plus a bonus on
  incremental contribution margin above an agreed baseline. Only with a client whose data you
  fully trust, whose attribution you built, and after at least one successful engagement.

**Never offer:** revenue-guarantee promises, "double your ROAS or free", or anything whose
outcome depends on a client behaviour you don't control (their stock, their product, their
pricing decisions).

## 7. Pricing conversation rules

- Never quote before the Teardown. "I could give you a number now, but it would be a guess,
  and you've had enough of those."
- Anchor against the cost of the current stack, not against other agencies' retainers.
- Sell the Teardown on the call. Sell the Sprint at the findings session, when they've just
  watched you find money in their business.
- Hold price. Reduce scope if the budget doesn't fit. Discounting the first invoice sets the
  ceiling on how the relationship is valued for its entire life.
- Minimum viable engagement: if they can't afford the Sprint, sell the Teardown and stop.
  A badly-funded engagement fails and becomes a bad reference.
