## The 30-day build order

Don't build all seven at once. This order gets you booking calls in week two.

**Week 1 — the ability to sell**
- [ ] Confirm pricing against your real delivery costs and margin (03)
- [ ] Choose one risk reversal and write the terms (03)
- [ ] Learn the call playbook — the frame and the reflect-back, verbatim (07)
- [ ] Build the outreach list: 200 qualified accounts (06)
- [ ] Warm a separate sending domain

**Week 2 — start conversations**
- [ ] Send outreach batch 1, 40/day (06)
- [ ] Record 5 free teardown videos for warm prospects — your highest-leverage asset
- [ ] Start LinkedIn: 4 posts/week from the 30 angles (06)
- [ ] Build the Teardown delivery template so you can fulfil what you're selling

**Week 3 — capture**
- [ ] Write and build the 40-point Growth Leak Audit (05)
- [ ] Record the 12-minute walkthrough on a permissioned real store (05)
- [ ] Build the 6-email nurture in Klaviyo (05)
- [ ] Simple landing page for the audit

**Week 4 — convert**
- [ ] Build the sales page (04) — fill or delete every `[[ ]]` placeholder
- [ ] Calendar embed with the five qualifying form fields (04)
- [ ] IG/TikTok: 3 videos + 2 carousels per week (06)
- [ ] Review week 1–4 numbers and set your own baselines

## Standing rules

1. **Never quote before the Teardown.** The diagnosis is the differentiator.
2. **Never invent proof.** Every number on a page, in an email or on a call must come from the
   prospect's own accounts or your own documented, permissioned client work. If you can't
   evidence it, delete it. This is a legal exposure and a reputational one, and in this market
   founders check.
3. **Publish your disqualifiers.** Saying who you don't work with converts better than any
   testimonial.
4. **Sell the outcome, deliver the execution.** Commercial improvement is the product; ads,
   sites and emails are the tools.
5. **Hold price, reduce scope.** Discounting the first invoice sets the value ceiling forever.
6. **Set baselines in writing before you change anything**, and report against them honestly
   whether they moved or not.
