Three leaks, and one thing you should protect.

Leak 01 · Measurement

59% of revenue is attributed to nothing at all

Critical · fix first

Over the last 180 days, Shopify attributes $1.01M of orders to a blank referrer source. Email — which is running ten live Klaviyo flows against a list that produces a 60% returning customer rate — is credited with 20 orders. Paid is credited with zero.

Email did not generate $3,087 in six months. Those orders exist; they're sitting in the blank row, because the links people click aren't carrying UTM parameters through to the order. The same is true of any paid traffic you're running.

This is the root cause of every other number in this report being hard to argue about. You cannot judge whether a channel is working, you cannot calculate a real blended MER, and you cannot tell whether Klaviyo's attributed revenue is incremental or double-counted against Meta's.

Every decision about where to spend money at SHE STREET is currently being made on 41% of the picture.

Leak 02 · Conversion

Conversion has fallen ~36%, and August's data is contaminated

Critical

Two separate things are happening here and they have to be untangled before anyone draws a conclusion.

First, the contamination. In August 2026, the direct / desktop segment recorded 87,867 sessions — five times July's level — and converted at 0.15%. That is not human traffic. It inflated August sessions to 191,881 and dragged the reported conversion rate down to 0.89%. It had largely stopped by September.

Second, the real decline. Strip the anomaly out and conversion is still falling: 2.37% in August 2025, 2.18% in March 2026, 1.72% in June, 1.53% in July, 1.52% in August, 1.34% so far in September. Sessions grew over the same period. You have been buying more traffic that converts less.

The leak is at the top of the funnel, not at checkout. Session-to-cart-addition fell from 8.6% to 3.7% year on year, while cart-to-checkout and checkout-completion stayed broadly flat. People are arriving and not engaging — which points at traffic quality and landing experience, not at your checkout.

Your checkout is fine. The problem is who is arriving, and what they land on.

Leak 03 · Margin

Discount rate drifted from 8.1% to 12.7%

High

Discounting as a share of gross sales averaged 8.07% across September–December 2025. Across April–July 2026 it averaged 12.74%, peaking at 15.2% in July. Revenue did not rise to match — July 2026 gross sales were $334,155 against March's $334,532, with $32,000 more given away to get there.

The Welcome flow offers 10% off and has not been edited since May 2025. A list that has been trained for sixteen months to expect a discount on entry will not buy at full price later, which is the mechanism by which a welcome offer quietly becomes a margin problem.

Holding discount rate at the 8.07% you achieved last spring, rather than the 12.74% of April–July, is worth about $159,000 a year in retained gross margin at current revenue.

Not a leak · protect this

Retention is the engine, and it is working

Strength

Returning customer rate has held between 55% and 66% every single month for twelve months, averaging about 60%. Roughly 14,500 of your 26,500 annual orders come from people who have bought before. That is a genuinely good number for apparel and it is the reason the business is stable while conversion has been falling.

New customer acquisition is what's flat: 10,101 new customers in twelve months, averaging 842 a month with no trend up. The business is being carried by people who already know you.

The risk here isn't that retention breaks. It's that discounting to hit revenue targets slowly erodes the margin on the 60% of orders that are currently your most profitable.

90-day roadmap

Sequenced, not simultaneous.

Three leaks fixed properly beats twelve started badly. Measurement first, because everything after it depends on the numbers being true.

Days 1–14

Make the numbers true

  • UTM tagging on every Klaviyo flow and campaign link, and on all paid placements
  • Identify and filter the direct/desktop bot traffic; add a filtered analytics view so future months aren't polluted
  • Re-baseline August and re-state any reporting that used it
  • Agree one reporting source of truth tied to blended MER and contribution margin

Days 15–45

Stop the margin bleed

  • Rebuild the Welcome flow — test a non-discount entry offer against the standing 10%
  • Audit every live discount code and its actual incremental contribution
  • Set a discount rate ceiling and report against it weekly
  • Archive the 17 draft and test flows cluttering Klaviyo; refresh Winback (untouched 3 years) and Product Review / Cross-Sell (untouched 4.5 years)

Days 46–75

Fix the top of the funnel

  • Diagnose the session-to-cart collapse by source, now that sources are trustworthy
  • Landing experience work on the pages taking social/mobile traffic — 1.21% CVR against search/mobile's 3.21% is the gap to close
  • Mobile PDP and load-speed pass; mobile is the overwhelming majority of real traffic
  • Structured conversion tests, two per month, sequenced by impact

Days 76–90

Compound and hand over

  • Results read-out against the day-14 baseline, reported honestly whether it moved or not
  • New-customer acquisition plan — the flat 842/month is the real ceiling on the business
  • Documented SOPs for the testing and reporting cadence

Scope

What this teardown could not see.

Honest limitations

  • Meta / paid ad accounts were not connected — no spend, CAC, ROAS or creative performance data. The acquisition pillar here is inferred from Shopify session and order data only, and Leak 01 means even that is partial.
  • No COGS or margin data — every figure is gross sales. Contribution margin, true CAC ceiling and LTV:CAC could not be calculated.
  • Klaviyo flow-level revenue not pulled — flow structure, status and staleness were audited; per-flow attributed revenue was not.
  • September 2026 is partial (11 days) and excluded from all trend calculations.
  • The bot-traffic conclusion is inference, not proof — 87,867 sessions converting at 0.15% is the signature of invalid traffic, but confirming it needs server log or GA4 review.
